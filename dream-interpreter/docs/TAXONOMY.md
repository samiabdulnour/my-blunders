# The Primordial Motif Taxonomy

This is the scaffolding for the Dreamweaver dictionary: a hierarchical
taxonomy of primordial motifs as identified in Jungian and comparative-
mythology scholarship — **Motif Family → Motif → Cultural Instantiations**.

Two rules govern it:

1. **Organize by image, never by meaning.** Every scholarly system that
   succeeds at open interpretation (ARAS / *The Book of Symbols*, Hillman's
   archetypal psychology, von Franz's amplification method) indexes images
   and narrative movements. Every system that fails (popular dream
   dictionaries) maps keyword → meaning.
2. **Families deliberately overlap.** A dream serpent belongs at once to
   the animal powers, the death-rebirth family, and the shadow family.
   The data model permits multi-parenting (see `SCHEMA.md`); single-parent
   trees force false disambiguation.

Cultural instantiations are **parallels for amplification, never
equivalences** — each widens the image; none closes it.

## Family 0 · Typical Dream Motifs (the phenomenological ground layer)

Jung's own inventory of "typical motifs" (CW 8, *On the Nature of Dreams*;
*Man and His Symbols*). These are *situations*, not symbols, and Jung
stressed they must not be interpreted by rote — they gain meaning only in
the dream's context and the dreamer's life. This family is the dictionary's
entry point for lay users, funneling into the deeper archetypal families.

- **Falling** — loss of standpoint; inflation compensated
- **Flying** — ascent, sublimatio; also dissociative escape
- **Being chased / pursued** — the split-off content demanding relationship
- **The examination / unprepared test** — the demand of the collective; being measured
- **Teeth falling out** — loss of grip/bite; life-stage transition (in many
  folk traditions also a death omen — record both valences)
- **Nudity / absurd clothing in public** — persona failure or persona shed
- **Being lost, in a crowd, in a hurry, missing the train** — ego disoriented amid the collective
- **Fighting with useless weapons / defenselessness** — impotence of the conscious attitude
- **Running and getting nowhere** — blocked libido
- **Paralysis / cannot scream** — overlaps sleep-paralysis phenomenology;
  incubus/mara folklore (Old Hag of Newfoundland, Norse mara, Italian
  Pandafeche) — a model case of culture-patterned dreaming
- **Growing infinitely large or small** — Jung: a typical infantile motif (cf. Alice)
- **The house with unknown rooms / new floors and cellars** — Jung's own
  house dream that seeded the collective-unconscious concept
- **Rising water / tidal wave** → Family 1
- **The snake in the path** → Family 11
- **Death of a loved one; one's own death** → Family 6
- **Discovering treasure / an unknown room** — the treasure hard to attain
- **Vehicles out of control** — how the dreamer is carried by collective or psychic energy

## Family 1 · Cosmos, Creation and Catastrophe

- **Primordial waters / pre-creation chaos** — Nun (Egypt), Tiamat–Apsu
  (Babylon), tehom (Genesis), Ginnungagap (Norse), the Ocean of Milk (India)
- **World egg** — Orphic Phanes, Pangu's egg (China), Brahmanda (India), the Kalevala teal's egg
- **Cosmic man / Anthropos** — Purusha (Rig Veda), Ymir (Norse), Pangu,
  Adam Kadmon (Kabbalah), the Gnostic Anthropos — Jung's key amplification
  for the Self projected onto the cosmos
- **Earth-diver creation** — Iroquois/Anishinaabe muskrat, Varaha the boar (India)
- **World Tree / axis mundi** — Yggdrasil, the Bodhi tree, the Tree of
  Sefirot, the Cross-as-tree, Irminsul, the Maya ceiba (Wacah Chan), the
  shamanic ascent-tree (Eliade), the inverted ashvattha of the Gita.
  Variants: world mountain (Meru, Kunlun, Olympus, Zion), world pillar,
  ladder (Jacob's, the Mithraic klimax), omphalos (Delphi)
- **The Flood** — Utnapishtim (Gilgamesh), Atrahasis/Ziusudra, Noah,
  Deucalion & Pyrrha, Manu and the fish, the Gun-Yu cycle (China),
  Unu Pachakuti (Inca), the Hopi emergence-flood. Psychologically:
  inundation by the unconscious; solutio preceding renewal
- **The sun's night journey** — Ra's barque through the Duat's twelve
  hours (the template Frobenius generalized), Helios' cup, Sól pursued by
  the wolf → Family 4
- **World conflagration / end and renewal** — Ragnarök, Stoic ekpyrosis,
  Kali Yuga, Revelation — enantiodromia at the collective scale

## Family 2 · The Great Mother (Neumann's schema)

Neumann's *The Great Mother* organizes the feminine archetype on two
bipolar axes — the **elementary character** (containing, bearing,
nourishing, holding fast) and the **transformative character** (driving
toward movement, change, ecstasy, spirit) — yielding four cardinal poles.
This four-pole diagram is generalized to all figure entries in the schema.

- **Good Mother (elementary +)** — Isis, Demeter, Gaia, Guanyin, the
  Madonna del Latte, Parvati, the Corn Mother, Ninhursag. Object
  correlates: vessel, bowl, oven, house, cave, cradle, breast, cow
  (Hathor, Audhumla), garden, spring
- **Terrible Mother (elementary −)** — Kali with the skull necklace,
  Gorgon, Rangda (Bali), Coatlicue and Tlaltecuhtli (Aztec), Hel, Baba
  Yaga (who is *also* donor — mark the ambivalence), Lilith, Lamia, the
  witch of Hansel and Gretel. Object correlates: the devouring maw,
  vagina dentata tales, spider, swamp, engulfing sea, sarcophagus
  ("flesh-eater"), tomb-as-womb
- **Positive transformative (Sophia pole)** — Sophia/Shekhinah, Beatrice,
  the Muses, Tara, the fairy godmother, Ariadne with the thread, Isis as
  initiatrix (Apuleius)
- **Negative transformative** — sirens, Lorelei, rusalki, Circe, Morgan
  le Fay, huldra, kitsune fox-wives, La Llorona

**Key principle to encode:** Neumann shows the *same symbol* (water, tree,
vessel, cave) migrates around all four poles depending on state and action
— the canonical argument for context-dependent valence in the data model.

## Family 3 · The Masculine Spirit Figures

- **Wise Old Man (senex +)** — Merlin, Tiresias, Chiron, Odin the
  wanderer, Elijah/Khidr (Sura 18 — Jung's favorite exemplum of the
  paradoxical guide), Laozi, Vyasa, the starets
- **Devouring father (senex −)** — Kronos–Saturn devouring his children,
  Uranus suppressing them, Herod, the ogre-king; the petrifying rule-giver
- **Sky Father** — Zeus, Jupiter, Tian, Ukko, Olorun, An/Anu
- **Wise Old Woman (distinct from Mother)** — the Sibyl, Hecate as guide,
  Grandmother Spider (Hopi/Navajo), the crone-donor of fairy tales

## Family 4 · The Hero Cycle and the Night Sea Journey

- **Frobenius' night-sea-journey template** (adopted by Jung in *Symbols
  of Transformation*): swallowed in the west by the sea-monster → eastward
  night-passage in the belly → fire kindled / heart cut out → hair lost
  (rebirth marker) → cuts free and emerges at dawn, often freeing all the
  previously swallowed. Instantiations: Jonah, Ra in the Duat, Heracles in
  the ketos, Hiawatha/Manabozho and the sturgeon Mishe-Nahma, Red Riding
  Hood (folk echo), Pinocchio (modern echo)
- **Campbell's monomyth** — 17 stages (Departure: Call, Refusal,
  Supernatural Aid, First Threshold, Belly of the Whale · Initiation:
  Road of Trials, Meeting with the Goddess, Temptress, Atonement with the
  Father, Apotheosis, Ultimate Boon · Return: Refusal of Return, Magic
  Flight, Rescue from Without, Return Threshold, Master of Two Worlds,
  Freedom to Live). Used as *narrative-position tags* on dreams, not as
  entry categories
- **Dragon fight / the treasure hard to attain** — Marduk vs Tiamat,
  Sigurd vs Fafnir, St George, Susanoo vs Yamata-no-Orochi, Indra vs
  Vritra, Perseus, Beowulf. Jung: the treasure is self-renewal won from
  the maternal dragon of the unconscious
- **Neumann's stages of consciousness** — uroboric containment →
  separation of the World Parents → dragon fight → captive-and-treasure →
  transformation — a developmental spine parallel to Campbell
- **Twin heroes** — Hunahpu & Xbalanque (Popol Vuh), the Ashvins,
  Romulus & Remus, Navajo Monster Slayer & Born-for-Water

## Family 5 · Descent to the Underworld (Katabasis)

The most richly instantiated narrative motif. Each descent has distinct
phenomenology and its own psychology:

- **Inanna/Ishtar** — stripped of one regalia at each of seven gates,
  hung on a hook, ransomed by a substitute (Dumuzi): descent as
  progressive divestment of persona
- **Persephone** — abduction, pomegranate seeds, cyclical return:
  descent as seizure and seasonal oscillation
- **Orpheus** — descent by art, the fatal backward look: the retrieval that fails
- **Izanagi seeking Izanami** — the broken prohibition against looking,
  flight, purification (misogi): descent ending in irreversible
  separation and cleansing
- **Ra's night journey** — the routinized, nightly descent; serpent-battle
  with Apophis; union of Ra and Osiris at midnight
- **Christ's Harrowing of Hell** — gates broken, the imprisoned freed:
  descent as redemption of the ancestors
- Also: **Gilgamesh** across the Waters of Death; **Odysseus'** nekyia;
  **Aeneas** with the golden bough; **Psyche's** errand to Persephone;
  **Hermod's** ride for Baldr; **Väinämöinen** in Tuonela; **Maui**
  entering Hine-nui-te-pō; **Hunahpu & Xbalanque** in Xibalba; shamanic
  soul-retrieval descents (Sedna at the sea floor)
- **Sub-motifs to index separately**: the gate series / divestment; the
  food-of-the-dead taboo; the backward look; the substitute/ransom; the
  guide; the return toll

## Family 6 · Death–Rebirth and Transformation (the Alchemical Family)

Two interlocking scholarly grids, both encoded as controlled vocabularies:

- **Color stages** (Jung, CW 12/14): **nigredo** (blackening —
  mortification, massa confusa), **albedo** (whitening — washing, dawning
  reflective light), **citrinitas** (yellowing — solar dawning), **rubedo**
  (reddening — embodiment, the Stone, coniunctio realized)
- **Edinger's seven operations** (*Anatomy of the Psyche*): **calcinatio**
  (fire — frustration of desire), **solutio** (water — dissolving,
  drowning, return to prima materia), **coagulatio** (earth — solidifying,
  incarnating), **sublimatio** (air — ascent, ladders, flight; danger of
  dissociation), **mortificatio** (death, rotting, dismemberment — the
  ego's defeat), **separatio** (cutting, sword, discrimination of
  opposites), **coniunctio** (union → Family 8). Dream images of
  fire/water/burial/flight carry an operation tag from this vocabulary
- **Dismemberment and re-membering** — Osiris (14 parts reassembled by
  Isis), Dionysus Zagreus, shamanic initiatory dismemberment and
  skeleton-vision (Eliade), sparagmos of Orpheus
- **Renewal creatures** — phoenix (with the Chinese fenghuang as
  parallel-not-equivalent), the skin-shedding snake, cicada,
  butterfly/psyche, Medea's cauldron, the Fountain of Youth

## Family 7 · Shadow and the Double

- **The double / Doppelgänger** — the Egyptian ka, the fetch (Ireland),
  fylgja (Norse), vardøger (Norway), the shadow sold or lost (Chamisso,
  Andersen's *The Shadow*), Dorian Gray, Jekyll/Hyde, the Zoroastrian
  daena met at the Chinvat bridge as one's own deeds embodied
- **Hostile brothers / dark twin** — Cain–Abel, Osiris–Set, Jacob–Esau,
  Ahura Mazda–Angra Mainyu; and Enkidu as wild double of Gilgamesh —
  the double as *completion*, not enemy (record both valences)
- **The pursuer in dreams** (→ Family 0) — Jung's first-approximation
  rule (same-sex figure as shadow) flagged as heuristic, never law

## Family 8 · Coniunctio, Syzygy and the Union of Opposites

- **Sacred marriage / hierosgamos** — Inanna–Dumuzi, Zeus–Hera,
  Shiva–Shakti, the Sol–Luna of the Rosarium Philosophorum (Jung's
  template in *Psychology of the Transference*), the Song of Songs
- **Syzygy** — the anima–animus pair; divine couples as projections
- **The Androgyne** — the alchemical Rebis, Plato's spherical primal
  humans, Ardhanarishvara, Hermaphroditus
- **Lesser vs greater coniunctio** (Edinger) — premature fusion that must
  die vs union of thoroughly separated opposites. Critical open-
  interpretation distinction: union images are not automatically "good"
- **Ouroboros** — pre-conscious totality (Neumann's uroboric stage) *and*
  the completed opus — same image, opposite developmental positions;
  the paradigm case for polarity fields

## Family 9 · The Divine Child and the Puer/Puella

- **The endangered child who survives** — Moses in the basket, Sargon,
  Karna, Perseus in the chest, Romulus & Remus, Zeus hidden in Crete,
  Krishna hidden from Kamsa, Horus hidden in the Delta marshes
- **The wonder-child** — infant Hermes stealing Apollo's cattle
  (trickster overlap), infant Heracles strangling serpents, Taliesin
- Jung/Kerényi: the child as futurity, "smaller than small yet bigger
  than big"; abandonment as precondition of the new. Encode: *the new
  thing in the psyche is always endangered*
- **Puer aeternus / puella** (von Franz) — Icarus, Peter Pan, the Little
  Prince: flight from coagulatio; shadow side of the child motif

## Family 10 · The Trickster

Radin/Kerényi/Jung's *The Trickster* as anchor text. Boundary-crosser,
sacred thief, culture-bringer-by-accident, shape- and sex-shifter:

- **Loki** (Norse) — shape/sex-shifter, engineer of Baldr's death *and*
  of the gods' treasures
- **Coyote** and **Wakdjunkaga** (Winnebago; Radin's cycle — trickster as
  evolving consciousness)
- **Raven** (Pacific NW) — steals the light for the world
- **Eshu/Elegba** (Yoruba/Fon) — crossroads god, message-scrambler, the
  two-colored hat that sets friends fighting; guardian of interpretive
  multiplicity itself
- **Hermes** — thief, herald, psychopomp, god of hermeneutics; the bridge
  between trickster and guide
- **Anansi** — the spider who owns all stories; **Maui**; **Susanoo**;
  **Sun Wukong**; Br'er Rabbit / Reynard / Till Eulenspiegel; Nasreddin
  Hodja (the wise-fool inversion)
- Psychological indexing: trickster as shadow of the collective, herald
  of change, compensator of rigidity — dreams of clowns, con-men,
  malfunctioning objects, absurdity

## Family 11 · Animal and Theriomorphic Powers

- **Serpent/Dragon** — the single most polyvalent motif: healing
  (Asclepius, Nehushtan), wisdom/kundalini, chaos-enemy (Apophis, Tiamat,
  Vritra), treasure-guardian, tempter, rainbow-serpent creator
  (Australia), Quetzalcoatl as feathered union-of-opposites, ouroboros.
  The mandatory demonstration entry for multi-valence
- **Bird** — soul-bird (ba), Garuda, the dove, the raven of prophecy and
  carrion, the owl of wisdom and death
- **Horse** — carrier of libido (Jung), psychopomp (Sleipnir), the
  nightmare/mara etymological cluster
- **Wolf/Dog** — devourer (Fenrir), guardian (Cerberus), guide (Xolotl
  leading through Mictlan; the dog at the Chinvat bridge)
- **Fish** — content of the deep, ichthys, Manu's fish, the salmon of wisdom
- **Spider** — weaver of fate/maya, Grandmother Spider creatrix, devouring
  centre of the web (Terrible Mother overlap), Anansi (trickster overlap)
- **Bull/Cow** — fertility, Hathor/Audhumla, the Minotaur at the
  labyrinth's centre, the Mithraic tauroctony
- **Bear** — initiatory death (hibernation-rebirth), Artemis' bear-maidens,
  the Ainu bear ceremony

## Family 12 · Threshold, Passage and the Guardian

- **Threshold guardians** — Cerberus, the Sphinx and her riddle, the
  cherubim with the flaming sword, Heimdall, dvarapalas, Niō guardians,
  Scylla & Charybdis, the troll under the bridge
- **The ferryman** — Charon, Urshanabi, the ford in fairy tales
- **Bridges of judgment** — the Chinvat bridge (wide for the just,
  blade-thin for the wicked), Bifröst, the sword bridge, the Sirat
- **Gates in series** — Inanna's seven, the Duat's twelve, the seven
  planetary gates of Mithraic ascent
- **Labyrinth** — Knossos, Chartres, the Hopi Tapu'at ("mother and
  child") — the path that is both prison and pilgrimage
- **Psychopomps** — Hermes, Anubis, Valkyries, Xolotl, Azrael, Vanth —
  the intra-psychic function that leads over
- **Liminal places as dream settings** — crossroads (Hecate, Eshu),
  doors, stairs, elevators, airports, border controls. Modern
  instantiations belong in the taxonomy: dreams update the wardrobe of
  the archetype

## Family 13 · Symbols of the Self and Totality

- **Mandala / quaternity** — Tibetan and Navajo sand mandalas, rose
  windows, the foursquare New Jerusalem, Ezekiel's four living creatures,
  the medicine wheel. Jung: spontaneous mandala production in dreams as
  ordering compensation in disorder
- **The Stone / lapis / pearl** — the philosopher's stone, the Hymn of
  the Pearl (Acts of Thomas — also a complete hero-descent narrative),
  the grail, the treasure hard to attain
- **Centre and circumference** — omphalos, the city, the temple, the
  hidden room at the centre of the house-dream
- **The cosmic man** (→ Family 1); God-images as Self-images
- **Numbers** — the 3→4 problem (the axiom of Maria: "one becomes two,
  two becomes three, and out of the third comes the one as the fourth"),
  7 (planetary stages), 12

## Structural models this taxonomy learns from

| System | Lesson taken | Trap avoided |
|---|---|---|
| **Thompson, Motif-Index of Folk-Literature** (23 alphabetic classes, stable IDs like F531) | A huge motif corpus is governable with stable IDs + hierarchy + cross-refs | Its classes mix actor, action and theme incoherently — we keep *image*, *narrative movement*, and *figure* as distinct record types |
| **ATU tale-type index** | Motifs (elements) and types (configurations) are different layers — we model both IMAGE-MOTIF and NARRATIVE-PATTERN records | Whole-plot typing alone loses the reusable element |
| **Hall/Van de Castle dream coding** (characters, interactions, emotions, settings, objects; norms at dreams.ucsc.edu) | A theory-neutral phenomenological description layer *before* any interpretation; enables empirical queries | Coding is not interpreting — HvdC stays a facet block, never the reading |
| **Warburg Institute classification** ("law of the good neighbour") | Browsing adjacency is an interpretive tool — entries surface curated unexpected neighbors | Search-only interfaces kill serendipity |
| **Iconclass** | Compositional notation lets one image carry multiple descriptions | Privileging one culture's material at the top level (its separate "Bible" class) — traditions are attributes of instantiations here, never top-level classes |
| **ARAS / The Book of Symbols** (Creation & Cosmos → Plant World → Animal World → Human World → Spirit World) | The entry genre is the *essay that circles the image* (Jung's circumambulatio), ordered on a phenomenological gradient | No explicit polarity fields, no dream-situation layer — added in our schema |
| **Von Franz's fairy-tale method** (exposition → dramatis personae → peripeteia → lysis) | Narrative-pattern entries encode start-configuration → end-configuration (the 3→4 move) | Amplification collapsing into equation — every parallel carries a divergence note |
