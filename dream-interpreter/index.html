    About Dreamweaver

    Dreamweaver is an open-source dream interpreter built on the analytical psychology of C. G. Jung. Write down a dream and it detects recurring motifs, maps them to archetypes — the deep structural patterns of the psyche — and amplifies them with parallels from ancient mythology, exactly as Jung recommended: understanding a dream image by holding it next to the myths where the same image has always lived.

    How to use it well

    • You are the authority. Jung insisted the dreamer's own felt sense is the final test of any interpretation. Everything here is a hypothesis to try on.
    • Dreams compensate. Ask what one-sided attitude of your waking life the dream might be balancing.
    • Context matters. Your mother in a dream is first of all your mother-image, and only then the Great Mother. Personal associations come before the dictionary.
    • No fortune telling. This is a tool for reflection, not prediction, and not a substitute for therapy or medical advice.

    Privacy

    Dreams are analyzed entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you explicitly use the AI deep-dive, which calls the Anthropic API directly with your own key.
